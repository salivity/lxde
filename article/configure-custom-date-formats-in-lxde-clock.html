Configure Custom Date Formats in LXDE Clock

This guide explains how to customize the date and time display on the LXDE desktop panel using standard formatting codes. By accessing the Digital Clock applet preferences, you can adjust the appearance to show day names, full dates, 12-hour or 24-hour time, and custom separators to suit your workflow.

Accessing the Clock Settings

  1. Right-click directly on the digital clock located in your LXDE panel (usually at the bottom-right corner of the screen).
  2. Select "Digital Clock" Settings from the context menu.
  3. In the preferences window that appears, locate the input field labeled Clock Format.

Customizing the Format String

The clock applet uses standard C strftime format codes. Enter your preferred combination of characters and specifiers into the Clock Format field.

Common formatting codes include:

  • %Y – Four-digit year (e.g., 2026)
  • %y – Two-digit year (e.g., 26)
  • %B – Full month name (e.g., October)
  • %b – Abbreviated month name (e.g., Oct)
  • %m – Two-digit month number (01 to 12)
  • %d – Two-digit day of the month (01 to 31)
  • %e – Day of the month with a leading space for single digits (1 to 31)
  • %A – Full weekday name (e.g., Monday)
  • %a – Abbreviated weekday name (e.g., Mon)
  • %H – Hour in 24-hour format (00 to 23)
  • %I – Hour in 12-hour format (01 to 12)
  • %M – Minute (00 to 59)
  • %S – Second (00 to 59)
  • %p – AM or PM indicator

Format Examples

You can combine these specifiers with spaces, commas, colons, or dashes:

  • Standard Date and 24-Hour Time:
    %Y-%m-%d %H:%M
    Output: 2026-03-30 14:45

  • Weekday, Date, and 12-Hour Time with AM/PM:
    %a, %b %d, %Y - %I:%M %p
    Output: Mon, Mar 30, 2026 - 02:45 PM

  • Minimalist Date and Time:
    %d/%m/%y %H:%M:%S
    Output: 30/03/26 14:45:00

Customizing the Tooltip

In the same settings dialog, you can modify the Tooltip Format field using the identical specifiers listed above. This controls what information appears when you hover your cursor over the clock. For example, setting the tooltip to %A, %B %d, %Y allows you to keep the panel clock compact while still checking the full date on hover.

Saving the Changes

Once you have entered your desired format strings, click Close. The panel will update immediately to reflect the new layout.
