How to Position LXDE Panel Vertically

The LXDE (Lightweight X11 Desktop Environment) desktop panel, known as LXPanel, fully supports vertical positioning on both the left and right edges of the screen. This article provides a concise guide on how to change the panel's orientation using the graphical settings menu, configure sizing for a vertical layout, and adjust applets to maintain a clean and functional interface.

Changing the Panel Position

To move the LXDE panel to the left or right edge:

  1. Right-click on any empty area of the panel.
  2. Select Panel Settings from the context menu.
  3. In the preferences window, open the Geometry tab.
  4. Locate the Edge setting and click the drop-down menu.
  5. Choose either Left or Right.
  6. Click Close to save and apply the changes immediately.

Adjusting Dimensions for a Vertical Layout

Once rotated, the measurement properties adapt to the new orientation:

  • Width: In vertical mode, this represents the length of the panel along the vertical edge. Set this to 100% if you want the panel to span the entire height of your screen, or set a lower percentage or pixel value for a floating dock appearance.
  • Height: In vertical mode, this represents the thickness (width) of the panel across the screen. A value between 24 and 48 pixels generally works best depending on your display resolution.

Optimizing Applets for Vertical Use

Certain panel plugins are designed primarily for horizontal layouts and may require tweaking when oriented vertically:

  • Taskbar (Windowed List): Long window titles can clutter a vertical bar. Open the Taskbar settings within the Panel Applets tab and enable icon-only mode if available, or reduce the maximum width allocated per window.
  • Digital Clock: Long date-and-time formats may wrap awkwardly. Modify the format string under the clock's settings to display a compact time format or split the time and date into separate lines.
  • System Tray: Notification icons automatically adjust, but ensure your panel thickness (the "Height" setting) is wide enough to display tray icons without clipping.
