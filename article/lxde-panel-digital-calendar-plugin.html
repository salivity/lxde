LXDE Panel Digital Calendar Plugin

This article identifies the specific plugin responsible for providing quick access to a digital calendar within the LXDE desktop panel and explains how to add, access, and configure it directly on your system.

The plugin that provides quick access to a digital calendar inside the LXDE panel (LXPanel) is the Digital Clock plugin. While its primary visible function on the panel is displaying the current time, clicking on the clock immediately opens a built-in, interactive digital calendar.

How the Calendar Works

By default, the Digital Clock applet sits in the system notification area of the LXDE panel. Left-clicking once on the displayed time triggers a compact calendar pop-up showing the current month and day. Users can navigate forward and backward through months and years using the arrow controls at the top of the calendar window. Clicking anywhere outside the calendar closes it.

Adding the Digital Clock to LXPanel

If the clock and calendar are missing from your panel, follow these steps to add the plugin:

  1. Right-click on any empty area of the LXDE panel.
  2. Select Add / Remove Panel Items from the context menu.
  3. In the Panel Preferences window, go to the Panel Applets tab.
  4. Click the Add button on the right side.
  5. Scroll through the list, select Digital Clock, and click Add.
  6. Use the Up and Down buttons to adjust the position of the clock on your panel.
  7. Click Close.

Customizing the Display

You can customize what information appears on the panel before clicking to view the calendar:

  1. Right-click the Digital Clock on the panel and select "Digital Clock" Settings.
  2. Modify the Clock Format field using standard strftime formatting codes (for example, %H:%M for a 24-hour clock or %A, %B %d, %Y to include the full date).
  3. Adjust the Tooltip format to change what displays when hovering the mouse over the clock.
  4. Click OK to apply changes.
