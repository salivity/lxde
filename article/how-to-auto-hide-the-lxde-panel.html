How to Auto-Hide the LXDE Panel

The LXDE desktop environment allows users to automatically hide the taskbar panel when the mouse cursor is not hovering over it. This article explains how to enable this feature through LXDE's built-in panel settings, helping you maximize available screen real estate without losing quick access to your application menu, system tray, and open windows.

To configure an LXDE panel (LXPanel) to auto-hide:

  1. Open Panel Settings: Right-click on an empty area of the panel you want to configure, then select Panel Settings from the context menu.
  2. Navigate to Advanced Options: In the configuration window that appears, click on the Advanced tab located at the top.
  3. Enable Auto-Hiding: Look for the option labeled Minimize panel when not in use and check the box next to it.
  4. Adjust Visibility (Optional): Below the minimize setting, you can adjust the Size when minimized value. Setting this to 1 or 2 pixels leaves a subtle edge for your mouse to trigger, while setting it to 0 makes it completely invisible until your cursor hits the screen border where the panel resides.
  5. Apply the Changes: Click Close to save your preferences.

Once enabled, the panel will automatically slide off-screen whenever your mouse cursor leaves its boundaries and will reappear instantly as soon as you move your cursor back to that edge of the monitor.
