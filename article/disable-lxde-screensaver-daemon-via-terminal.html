Disable LXDE Screensaver Daemon via Terminal

This guide demonstrates how to temporarily disable the screensaver daemon in the Lightweight X11 Desktop Environment (LXDE) using simple terminal commands. You will learn how to pause or stop the underlying xscreensaver process as well as how to disable the X11 power management settings to prevent your display from dimming or locking during critical tasks.

1. Pause and Resume the Daemon

The quickest way to temporarily disable xscreensaver without killing the process entirely is to send a stop signal using pkill. This freezes the screensaver daemon in memory, preventing it from tracking idle time or launching screensavers.

To temporarily pause the screensaver:

pkill -STOP xscreensaver

When you are ready to re-enable the daemon and restore normal screen-locking behavior, resume the process:

pkill -CONT xscreensaver

2. Stop the Daemon Using xscreensaver-command

If you prefer to cleanly terminate the screensaver process, you can use the built-in control utility xscreensaver-command.

To turn off the daemon:

xscreensaver-command -exit

To restart the screensaver daemon in the background when you are finished:

xscreensaver -no-splash &

3. Disable X11 DPMS and Screen Blanking

In some LXDE setups, the underlying X server's Display Power Management Signaling (DPMS) may still blank the screen even if the screensaver daemon is stopped. To prevent the screen from turning off completely, disable X11 blanking using xset.

To turn off screen blanking and DPMS:

xset s off
xset -dpms

To restore the default display power management and blanking behaviors later:

xset s on
xset +dpms
